What does it actually do?

It gives every employee instant answers from your own knowledge base: allergens, menu items, payment options, HR policies. No searching or skimming. They ask, and they get an answer in seconds.

Is it your own language model?

No. All Gravy sits on top of the most powerful models available and designs the interface that makes them useful for everyday hospitality work.

What if the AI can't answer?

It escalates to a human, and you're notified about the missing information so you can add it. The next person who asks gets an answer.

Can it do tasks, not just answer?

Agentic features are on the way: things like swapping shifts or creating content for you. Answering comes first; doing comes next.

How is our data kept safe?

All Gravy is GDPR-compliant and ISAE 3000 certified by BDO Denmark, with encryption and regular third-party audits.
